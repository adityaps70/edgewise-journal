The fields that matter

The trade

  • Date and time (and session: London, New York AM, etc.)
  • Instrument and direction: NQ long, EURUSD short
  • Entry, stop, target, exit
  • Size and P&L in dollars and in R (P&L ÷ initial risk)

The decision

  • Setup name: "opening range break", "VWAP reclaim"
  • Grade: A+, B or C, assigned before entry
  • Reason: one sentence on why
  • Followed plan? yes / no, and if no, what broke

The trader

  • State before entry: calm, bored, rushed, angry, FOMO
  • Mindset score 0–10: how detached and patient you were
  • Exit quality: planned exit, early fear exit, moved stop

For prop firm accounts, add

  • Account and firm
  • Room left after the trade (balance minus drawdown floor; see trailing drawdown explained)
  • Day's P&L vs the consistency limit (see consistency rule)

Daily: before and after

Before the open (3 minutes): key levels, news times, max loss for the day, which setups you're allowed to take.

After the close (5 minutes): did you follow the daily stop, best and worst decision, mindset score, room left for tomorrow.

Weekly review: where the edge comes from

  1. Total P&L by setup: which ones pay and which leak?
  2. P&L by grade: do A+ trades really outperform C trades?
  3. P&L by time of day and state: when and how do you lose?
  4. Pick one rule for next week, e.g. "stop after two losses" or "no C setups".
