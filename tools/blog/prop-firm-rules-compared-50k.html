Topstep vs Apex Trader Funding

These two are the most-searched pair, so here is the short version from the data above:

  • Billing: Topstep's Combine is a monthly subscription; Apex is a one-time evaluation fee.
  • Drawdown: Topstep uses end-of-day trailing. Apex sells both EOD and intraday trailing versions; the intraday one trails your open profit (see our trailing drawdown guide).
  • Consistency: Topstep's Combine has a 50% rule; Apex's evaluation has none listed in our catalog.
  • Passing costs: Topstep charges a $149 activation fee; Apex shows $0.
  • Daily loss: no default firm daily limit on TopstepX (you can add one); none listed for Apex either. Both reward a personal daily stop.

The things the table can't show

  1. Payout rules. Minimum days, payout caps, buffers and funded-stage consistency differ more than evaluation rules and change more often.
  2. Resets. A cheap evaluation you reset three times costs more than an expensive one you pass first time.
  3. Your style. If you hold through pullbacks, intraday trailing will hurt. If you have one big day a week, a 40–50% consistency rule will slow you down.
