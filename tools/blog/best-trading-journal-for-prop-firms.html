What a prop firm trader actually needs

A general trading journal answers "am I profitable?". A prop firm trader also needs to answer:

  • How far is each account from breaching, and from passing?
  • Am I inside the consistency rule and the daily limit?
  • Across all my firms, are payouts beating fees?
  • Which setups and states of mind are costing me money?

Where each one fits

TradeZella

Polished, popular, with lots of prop firm content and tools. No free plan, so the cost adds up next to evaluation fees. Good if you want an established product and don't mind paying.

TradesViz

Very deep analytics and a genuinely useful free plan, already priced in rupees. The interface is dense; it suits traders who like to dig through reports.

Tradervue

One of the oldest journals, with solid futures imports. Prop-specific features are limited, so drawdown and firm rules are mostly on you.

TraderSync and Edgewonk

Strong general journals: TraderSync leans on AI analysis, Edgewonk on psychology and discipline. Neither is built around multiple prop accounts.
