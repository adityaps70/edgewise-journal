Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The Smart Trade (thesmarttrade.in) is an online trading journal operated by Aditya Pratap Singh, India. This page explains how paying for Pro, cancelling and refunds work.

What you pay for

The free plan includes 10 trade logs and 10 AI coach requests. Pro costs ₹999 a month in India, or US$20 a month elsewhere, paid in advance, and removes those limits. Prices are shown on the pricing section before you pay. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay; we never see or store your full card details.

Delivery

The Smart Trade is a digital service. Nothing is shipped. Pro is switched on for your account immediately after a successful payment, and you'll see it in the app under Settings. If Pro isn't active within 30 minutes of paying, email us and we'll fix it the same day.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Settings or by emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges. Pro stays active until the end of the month you've already paid for, and your journal and data stay in your account on the free plan afterwards.

Refunds

  • First payment: if Pro isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full.
  • Charged by mistake: duplicate charges, or a charge after you cancelled, are always refunded in full.
  • Service problems: if Pro was unavailable for a significant part of a paid month because of a fault on our side, we'll refund that month.
  • Other renewals aren't refunded, but you can cancel at any time so you won't be charged again.

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

How to ask

Email support@thesmarttrade.in from the email you sign in with and include the payment date. We reply within 2 working days.
