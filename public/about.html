About The Smart Trade

The Smart Trade is a trading journal built for prop firm traders: several accounts, each with its own rules, fees and payouts.

Who builds it

Aditya Pratap Singh trades futures evaluations and funded accounts and builds The Smart Trade. The goal is simple: one place that shows how close every account is to breaching, and whether the fees you've paid are actually coming back as payouts.

What we believe

  • Know your room. The distance to your drawdown floor matters more than your P&L.
  • Count every fee. Failed evaluations, resets and activation fees are part of the real cost of prop trading.
  • Rules come from the source. Our firm rules are checked against each firm's public pages and dated. Every value stays editable because firms change rules often.
  • No firm deals. We aren't affiliated with any prop firm and don't earn commission from them.

How the blog is written

Our guides explain prop firm rules and journaling in plain English. Firm numbers come from the same dated rules catalog the app uses, and articles are reviewed and updated when rules change. Nothing here is financial advice.
