FAQ

Good to know

Is it free?/01

Yes, while it's in beta. Create a free account and start logging; the AI coach is included with a daily limit.

Where is my data stored?/02

In your private account, on Cloudflare's network. Only you can see it, unless you create a mentor link, and even then payouts and costs stay private. Export a full backup any time from Settings.

Which prop firms are included?/03

Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, TopOne Futures, Tradeify, MyFundedFutures, Lucid Trading, Take Profit Trader, FTMO and FundingPips, plus any other firm whose rules you enter yourself.

Are the firm rules always current?/04

They were checked against public sources in November 2026, but firms change rules often. Each account links to the firm's site and every value can be edited.

Is The Smart Trade affiliated with any prop firm?/05

No. The Smart Trade is an independent journal and doesn't sell or promote any firm's accounts.
