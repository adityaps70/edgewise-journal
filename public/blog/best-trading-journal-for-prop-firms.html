Best trading journal for prop firm traders in 2026: free and paid options compared

By Aditya Pratap Singh · 2026-11-23 · 7 min read

Most trading journals assume one brokerage account. Prop traders juggle several, each with its own rules, fees and payouts. This is how the popular journals handle that, including where ours falls short.

What a prop firm trader actually needs

A general trading journal answers "am I profitable?". A prop firm trader also needs to answer:

  • How far is each account from breaching, and from passing?
  • Am I inside the consistency rule and the daily limit?
  • Across all my firms, are payouts beating fees?
  • Which setups and states of mind are costing me money?

Where each one fits

TradeZella

Polished, popular, with lots of prop firm content and tools. No free plan, so the cost adds up next to evaluation fees. Good if you want an established product and don't mind paying.

TradesViz

Very deep analytics and a genuinely useful free plan, already priced in rupees. The interface is dense; it suits traders who like to dig through reports.

Tradervue

One of the oldest journals, with solid futures imports. Prop-specific features are limited, so drawdown and firm rules are mostly on you.

TraderSync and Edgewonk

Strong general journals: TraderSync leans on AI analysis, Edgewonk on psychology and discipline. Neither is built around multiple prop accounts.

The Smart Trade (ours)

How to choose

  1. One personal account, deep stats: TradesViz (free) or Tradervue.
  2. Several prop accounts on a budget: The Smart Trade (free plan, Pro $20/mo) or TradesViz free.
  3. Want the most established paid tool: TradeZella.

FAQ

What is the best free trading journal for prop firms?

Free options include TradesViz's free plan, Tradervue's free plan (limited trades per month) and The Smart Trade, which has a free plan (10 trades) and is built around prop firm accounts, drawdown and payouts. TradeZella and TraderSync have no free plan.

Is there a free alternative to TradeZella?

Yes. TradesViz has a free plan with broad analytics, and The Smart Trade is free during its beta and focuses on prop firm rules, per-account drawdown and payout tracking. Spreadsheets also work but take more maintenance.

What should a prop firm trader look for in a trading journal?

Per-account drawdown and profit-target tracking, firm rule presets, consistency checks, CSV import from your platform (Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic, TopstepX, MT4/MT5), payout and fee tracking per firm, and a way to review mistakes by setup and emotion.
