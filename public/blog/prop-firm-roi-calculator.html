Prop firm ROI: are your challenges actually profitable?

2026-11-23 · 6 min read

Payout screenshots are everywhere. What nobody posts is the stack of failed evaluations, resets and activation fees behind them. Here is how to calculate whether prop trading is actually paying you.

The formula

Net = payouts received − (evaluation fees + resets + subscriptions + activation fees + data fees)
ROI = Net ÷ total paid

Two rules make this honest:

  • Count payouts when they hit your bank, not when requested, and at the amount you actually received after transfer and conversion fees.
  • Count every fee, including evaluations you failed. Breached accounts are the biggest hidden cost in prop trading.

Why most traders overestimate their prop firm returns

  1. Failed evaluations fade from memory. A $49 evaluation feels free; twelve of them in a year are $588.
  2. Monthly billing keeps running. Subscription-style evaluations bill until you pass or cancel.
  3. Activation fees arrive at the happiest moment, right after you pass, so they rarely get counted.
  4. Conversion costs are invisible. For traders in India, the gap between the market rate and what reaches your bank account can be a few percent of every payout. Record the rupees received, not the dollar headline.

Track ROI per firm, not overall

One firm might be paying for all the others. Split your fees and payouts by firm and you'll often find one firm you pass easily and one you keep feeding. FAQ

How do you calculate prop firm ROI?

Add up everything you paid the firm: evaluation fees, resets, monthly subscriptions, activation fees and data fees. Subtract that from the payouts you actually received. Divide the result by the total paid. If you paid $600 and received $900, your net is $300 and your ROI is 50%.

Is prop firm trading worth it?

It is worth it only if your total payouts exceed your total fees by enough to justify the time. Many traders lose money on fees without realising, because they track payouts but not resets and activation fees. Track both per firm for a few months before deciding.

How do I track prop firm payouts in rupees?

Record each payout in USD along with the rupee amount that actually reached your bank after conversion and transfer fees. The difference between the market rate and what you received is a real cost. Our journal converts payouts at the live rate and lets you record the actual amount received.
