How to read it: "Daily limit" is the firm's daily loss limit, where one exists; "None" means the evaluation has no firm daily loss limit, so set your own. "Activation" is the one-time fee some firms charge when you pass. "Split" is your share of profits once funded.

Topstep vs Apex Trader Funding

This is the comparison people search for most. From the table above:

  • Billing: Topstep's Combine is a monthly subscription; Apex is a one-time evaluation fee.
  • Drawdown: Topstep uses end-of-day trailing. Apex sells both EOD and intraday trailing versions; the intraday one trails your open profit (see our trailing drawdown guide).
  • Consistency: Topstep's Combine has a 50% rule; Apex's evaluation has none listed in our catalog.
  • Passing costs: Topstep charges a $149 activation fee; Apex shows $0.
  • Daily loss: no default firm daily limit on TopstepX (you can add one); none listed for Apex either. Both reward a personal daily stop.

The things the table can't show

  1. Payout rules. Minimum days, payout caps, buffers and funded-stage consistency differ more than evaluation rules and change more often.
  2. Resets. A cheap evaluation you reset three times costs more than an expensive one you pass first time.
  3. Your style. If you hold through pullbacks, intraday trailing will hurt. If you have one big day a week, a 40–50% consistency rule will slow you down.

FAQ

Is Topstep or Apex better?

It depends on how you trade. In our November 2026 data, Apex's 50K EOD evaluation is a one-time fee with no activation fee and no evaluation consistency rule, while Topstep's 50K Combine is billed monthly, has a 50% consistency rule and a $149 activation fee, but no default daily loss limit on TopstepX. Intraday scalpers who give back open profit often prefer EOD accounts.

What are the Apex Trader Funding 50K rules?

In our catalog, the Apex 50K evaluation has a $3,000 profit target and a $2,000 trailing drawdown, with a choice of end-of-day or intraday trailing, a 1-day minimum and a 90% split. Check Apex's site for current pricing and payout rules.

Which futures prop firm is cheapest?

On sticker price for a 50K evaluation in our data, Apex and TopOne Elite Access are the cheapest one-time fees. The real cost depends on resets, activation fees and how many attempts you need, so track total spend against payouts.
