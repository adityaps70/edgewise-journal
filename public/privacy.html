Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The Smart Trade (thesmarttrade.in) is a trading journal. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. In short: your journal is yours, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and nothing else.
  • Journal data you enter or import: trades, accounts, payouts, notes, journal entries, screenshots, backtests and settings.
  • Data from platforms you connect: if you connect a trading platform (for example through an API key or our MetaTrader or NinjaTrader add-ons), we receive your trade history from it. API keys you give us are stored encrypted and used only to fetch your trades.
  • Basic technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to run the journal for you: storing your data, calculating your statistics, and powering features you choose to use.

AI coach: when you use an AI feature, the relevant part of your journal is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to generate the answer. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models under its commercial terms.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with the services that run the product: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Anthropic (AI features, only when you use them), and Google (only to sign you in, if you choose Google sign-in). If you create a mentor link, the person you send it to can see your trades and stats. Payouts and costs are never included, and you can turn a link off at any time.

Your choices

  • Export all your data from Settings at any time.
  • Delete all your journal data from Settings. To delete your account entirely, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
  • Disconnect any trading platform or mentor link whenever you like.

Security

Data is stored on Cloudflare's network and sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect.
